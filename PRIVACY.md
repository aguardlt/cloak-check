# Privacy policy

CloakCheck does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- The extension runs only when you click its icon.
- It requests the address of the tab you are checking, several times, directly from that website. These requests carry no cookies.
- It reads the content of that tab to look for hidden links. The result is shown in the popup and discarded when the popup closes.
- Nothing is sent to AGuard or to any third party. The extension has no server, no analytics and loads no remote code.
- "Copy report" places the text on your clipboard. It goes nowhere unless you paste it.
