# Privacy policy

CloakCheck does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- The extension acts on a site only after you allow that site. On allowed sites it checks each page you open for hidden links, locally, and shows the count on its icon.
- It requests the address of the tab you are checking, several times, directly from that website. These requests carry no cookies.
- It reads the content of the tab to look for hidden links. The result is shown on the icon and in the popup and is not stored.
- If you switch a tab to a crawler signature, the User-Agent and Referer headers of that tab are changed until you switch back or close it.
- Nothing is sent to AGuard or to any third party. The extension has no server, no analytics and loads no remote code.
- "Copy report" places the text on your clipboard. It goes nowhere unless you paste it.
